Meet The Maker

The Journey of Knot & Nest by JP

Knot & Nest was born out of a deep reverence for traditional slow-craft traditions and mindful living. What began as quiet evening stitching with pure cotton yarns turned into a heartfelt mission: to replace disposable, mass-produced items with pieces that carry soul, texture, and longevity.

As an independent artisan in India, JP designs and hand-crochets each piece individually. There are no conveyor belts, no synthetic shortcuts, and no rush. Every loop, knot, and seam is carefully guided by hand.

When you welcome a Knot & Nest piece into your home or carry it on your shoulder, you are holding hours of dedicated focus, high-grade natural materials, and an authentic celebration of Indian handmade culture.

Artisanal Method

Our Mindful Handcrafting Process

Transparency is at the heart of our studio. Here is how raw skeins of yarn transform into your cherished finished piece.

01

Conscious Fiber Selection

We source only high-grade ring-spun cotton, breathable organic bamboo, and baby-soft anti-pilling merino wool from ethical suppliers.

02

Stitch by Stitch Crafting

Using ergonomic wooden hooks, each piece is shaped with intentional stitch tension to ensure shape-holding strength and balanced drape.

03

Quality Finishing & Linings

Every loose yarn end is invisibly woven in. Bags and clutches receive hand-tailored natural muslin linings and reinforced handles.

04

Eco-Conscious Packaging

Your piece is wrapped in recycled kraft paper, tied with organic jute twine, and accompanied by a handwritten care note from JP.

Quality Philosophy

Built For Longevity, Not Landfills

We believe true luxury is found in items made with care that last for years. We rigorously test our yarn choices for colorfastness, anti-fraying resilience, and ease of washing.

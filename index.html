Our Philosophy

Slow, Intentional Craft Over Mass Production

In a fast-paced world flooded with mass-produced synthetic items, Knot & Nest by JP is a tribute to the quiet joy of slow, intentional hand-craftsmanship.

Founded by crochet artist JP, every single creation begins with carefully curated natural fibers—from pure Indian combed cotton to hypoallergenic bamboo. No hurried automated machines, no plastic shortcuts. Just one hook, exquisite yarn, and hours of mindful dedication.
